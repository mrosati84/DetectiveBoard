Interrogation room

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. DetectiveBoard is free to use, with no ads and no premium tier.

What about my privacy?

Your boards are private to your account. We don’t use cookies or ads; we only keep anonymous, aggregate visit counts so we know the site is being used.

Can other people see my boards?

Only if you share one. Sharing creates a read-only link that you can disable at any time.

Does it work on my phone?

DetectiveBoard runs in any modern browser, though a bigger screen gives your investigation more room to breathe.
